## The entry

One funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its human — under 200 characters, deadpan shape of a real review, with the complaint going somewhere only a cat would go:

> One star. He locks me out of the bathroom. I have seen him naked. There is nothing left to hide, Kevin.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt`, asserting it is a single line of at most 200 characters and keeps the deadpan one-star review shape.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
